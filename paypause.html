Privacy policy

PayPause helps you record subscriptions and free trials and schedule reminders. It does not connect to banks, scan inboxes or read other apps in the background. You do not need a PayPause account.

Information you choose to provide

Subscription names, prices, dates, notes and optional cancellation screenshots are saved on your device. Selected screenshot recognition uses Apple Vision on device. Voice recognition is used only when you request it and the selected language supports on-device recognition; otherwise use manual entry or a screenshot. Audio is temporarily recorded on your device, transcribed on device after you stop, and deleted after completion, cancellation or failure. Interrupted temporary recordings are cleared on the next app launch. Audio is not uploaded or included in backups.

Private iCloud synchronization

Eligible Pro users can enable synchronization with their private iCloud database. Records and selected cancellation proof can then be stored in iCloud and synchronized between their devices. Apple's iCloud terms and storage availability apply. Turning sync off keeps local records; changes may already exist in your private iCloud.

Purchases and notifications

Apple handles purchases and billing. PayPause checks verified purchase information to determine Pro access and can maintain a reminder for its own subscription. Notification text may be visible on your device; use the amount-hiding option and system notification settings as appropriate. Notification delivery is controlled by iOS and your settings.

Tracking and third parties

No advertising, external analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDK is included. Opening a provider's cancellation or refund website uses that provider's service and privacy policy. Sharing an export or support request sends information only through the destination you choose.

Your controls

You can edit or delete records and cancellation proof, turn notifications and optional sync off, and export or restore a local backup. Backups may contain private subscription information and images; keep them private. Sync deletions and recoverable conflict copies require attention on your other devices. Device security and App Lock complement these controls.

Support

Reminders are missing

Open Settings in PayPause and check notification status. Enable notifications in iPhone Settings, inspect Focus and notification summaries, and send a test reminder. Open PayPause periodically to refresh its finite future notification queue.

Cancel a subscription

Open a record and check its purchase channel. Follow the official management link for Apple, Google Play or the provider. PayPause does not cancel the subscription itself. After completing the provider's cancellation, confirm its status and access end date, then update the record.

Restore Pro

Use Restore Purchases in PayPause Settings or on the Pro screen, with the Apple Account used for purchase. A one-time lifetime unlock does not itself cancel an existing monthly or annual subscription.

Move to another phone

Export a backup before switching devices and restore it in PayPause. Optional Pro iCloud sync requires the correct iCloud account and available storage. Keep an independent backup for recovery from accidental deletion.

Contact

Email paypause.support@proton.me or use Settings → Help and feedback in PayPause. You choose what to write and confirm sending in your email app. App-generated drafts include your message, feedback category, app version and iOS version; no subscription records, receipts or recordings are attached automatically. Do not include payment credentials, passwords or complete private receipts in support requests.

Review invitations

PayPause keeps a small local record of first use, distinct usage days, completed subscription actions and review-request timing to avoid asking too early or too often. This information remains on your device and is not sent to the developer or included in subscription backups. Feedback and App Store reviews are available independently.
