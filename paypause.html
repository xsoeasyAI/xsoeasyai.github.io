Privacy policy

PayPause helps you record subscriptions and free trials and schedule reminders. It does not connect to banks, scan inboxes or read other apps in the background. You do not need a PayPause account.

Information you choose to provide

Subscription names, prices, dates, notes and optional cancellation screenshots are saved on your device. Selected screenshot recognition uses Apple Vision on device. Voice recognition is used only when you request it and the selected language supports on-device recognition; otherwise use manual entry or a screenshot. Audio is temporarily recorded on your device, transcribed on device after you stop, and deleted after completion, cancellation or failure. Interrupted temporary recordings are cleared on the next app launch. Audio is not uploaded or included in backups.

Device-local free experience

The first opening of PayPause on this device starts a 7-day (168-hour) experience. Its start and most recent use times are stored locally, including in the device Keychain so a normal uninstall and reinstall does not restart the experience. This timing information is not sent to the developer, synchronized across devices or included in subscription backups. No PayPause account is required.

Private iCloud synchronization

During the free experience or with verified Pro access, you can enable synchronization with your private iCloud database. Records and selected cancellation proof can then be stored in iCloud and synchronized between your devices. Apple's iCloud terms and storage availability apply. When the experience ends without Pro, synchronization pauses and local records remain. Turning sync off keeps local records; changes may already exist in your private iCloud.

Purchases and notifications

The local PayPause experience does not start an Apple purchase and does not automatically charge you. It is separate from any Apple introductory offer. If you choose a monthly or yearly purchase, you start an auto-renewing Apple subscription; the purchase screen shows current local prices and any eligible offer before you confirm. Apple handles purchases, billing and renewals. A lifetime purchase is a one-time purchase. PayPause checks verified purchase information to determine Pro access and can maintain a reminder for its own subscription. Notification text may be visible on your device; use the amount-hiding option and system notification settings as appropriate. Notification delivery is controlled by iOS and your settings.

Tracking and third parties

No advertising, external analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDK is included. Opening a provider's cancellation or refund website uses that provider's service and privacy policy. Sharing an export or support request sends information only through the destination you choose.

Your controls

You can view records, export CSV and local backups, mark renewal stopped, delete records and undo those actions even after the free experience ends. Existing reminders continue. You can turn notifications and optional sync off. Adding or editing records, synchronizing with iCloud and displaying widget amounts require an active experience or verified Pro access. Backup restore remains available for data recovery; it does not restart the experience or unlock paid editing. Backups may contain private subscription information and images; keep them private. Sync deletions and recoverable conflict copies require attention on your other devices. Device security and App Lock complement these controls.

Support

What is included in the free experience?

For 168 hours from your first opening on this device, you can use all features with no limit on subscription records: add and edit records, choose reminder dates, times and counts, enable optional private iCloud sync, and show amounts in widgets. No purchase is started and there is no automatic charge. The experience status is local to this device and does not restart after a normal reinstall.

What happens when it ends?

Your records remain available to view. You can always export CSV and local backups, mark renewal stopped, delete records and undo those actions. Already-recorded reminders continue; their delivery still depends on iPhone settings and permissions. Backup restore remains available for data recovery without Pro. Restoring does not restart your experience or unlock adding or editing. To add or edit records, change reminder dates or times, use iCloud sync or display widget amounts, choose Pro. Merely reaching the end of the experience does not start an Apple subscription or charge you.

How is this different from an Apple subscription trial?

The device-local PayPause experience begins when you first open the app. An eligible Apple introductory offer applies only to the Apple subscription purchase you explicitly choose. Monthly and yearly purchases automatically renew under the terms shown by Apple; manage or cancel them in Apple Account → Subscriptions. Check the purchase screen for current local prices and eligible offers. The local experience does not change your existing Apple subscription or its renewal terms.

Reminders are missing

Open Settings in PayPause and check notification status. Enable notifications in iPhone Settings, inspect Focus and notification summaries, and send a test reminder. Open PayPause periodically to refresh its finite future notification queue.

Cancel a subscription

Open a record and check its purchase channel. Follow the official management link for Apple, Google Play or the provider. PayPause does not cancel the subscription itself. After completing the provider's cancellation, confirm its status and access end date, then update the record.

Restore Pro

Use Restore Purchases in PayPause Settings or on the Pro screen, with the Apple Account used for purchase. A one-time lifetime unlock does not itself cancel an existing monthly or annual subscription.

Back up and restore

Export backup saves a folder containing a manifest.json file and a receipts folder. Keep the entire folder together in a private location. To restore, select that folder in PayPause; older JSON backups are also accepted. CSV is a spreadsheet export and cannot restore receipt images. PayPause checks the backup before merging it with your current records.

Move to another phone

You can export a CSV or local backup at any time, including after your experience ends. Export a backup before switching devices. You can restore a backup for data recovery even after the experience ends, without Pro. Restoring does not restart the experience or unlock adding or editing. Optional private iCloud sync is available during the experience and with Pro, and requires the correct iCloud account and available storage. The experience timing does not transfer with your subscription backup or synchronize through iCloud. Keep an independent backup for recovery from accidental deletion.

Contact

Email paypause.support@proton.me or use Settings → Help and feedback in PayPause. You choose what to write and confirm sending in your email app. App-generated drafts include your message, feedback category, app version and iOS version; no subscription records, receipts or recordings are attached automatically. Do not include payment credentials, passwords or complete private receipts in support requests.

Review invitations

PayPause keeps a small local record of first use, distinct usage days, completed subscription actions and review-request timing to avoid asking too early or too often. This information remains on your device and is not sent to the developer or included in subscription backups. Feedback and App Store reviews are available independently.
